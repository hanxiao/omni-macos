# Linked Macs - storyboard

One minute, 1920x1080, 60 fps. The launch film's world (cut paper, the mole, Sadaltager at 1.1x,
a Lyria score), real app footage for every claim about the app. Times are approximate until the
narration is placed; each beat says what the viewer learns.

| # | Time | Narration | Picture | The viewer learns |
|---|------|-----------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | 0-5 | "Your files live on more than one Mac." | Paper plate: two desks, a laptop and a desktop Mac, files scattered on each; under each desk a mole in its own burrow, the burrows apart. | The problem: files are split across Macs. |
| 2 | 5-14 | "Omni can now link them. Open Link a Mac on both, and type the six digits one shows into the other." | Two paper laptops; their screens are the real Link a Mac sheets. Left: "Enter this code on MacBook Pro" with the digits; right: "Link with Mac Studio?", the digits typed in one at a time, then "Linked with MacBook Pro". | How to link: one window on each Mac, six digits. |
| 3 | 14-24 | "The code proves the two Macs are talking to each other, with nothing in between. No server is involved: they find each other on your network, or over your VPN." | Underground: a tunnel is dug between the two burrows, a teal thread runs through it, a paper seal stamps onto it. A cloud above drifts in and is cut away. Chips: "TLS 1.3", "keys pinned at pairing", "no server". | It is private and needs no cloud. |
| 4 | 24-36 | "From then on, one search covers every linked Mac. Each one searches its own index on its own GPU, and its results join yours as they arrive." | A query card travels the tunnel; the far mole digs; photos travel back. Push into the real app: a search where MacBook Pro's photos land among this Mac's, rows reading "MacBook Pro > ~/Pictures/Iceland". | One search, every Mac, each on its own compute. |
| 5 | 36-41 | "If a Mac is asleep, the search simply goes on without it." | The score breaks down. The far mole curls up asleep, its screen dims; the near mole keeps working, results still arrive. | An offline Mac never blocks a search. |
| 6 | 41-48 | "Open, preview or drag a result, and the file comes across the link. Its folders are in your sidebar." | The groove returns at 46.3 as the far mole wakes. Real app: the sidebar's Linked Macs section, MacBook Pro selected, its folder browsed; a photo travels across the tunnel into the laptop. | Remote files behave like local ones. |
| 7 | 49-53 | "Local agents get the same reach, through the same tools." | A terminal card: `omni search "mountains at sunset"`, its output with an "On MacBook Pro:" section, typed in. | Agents and the command line see linked Macs too. |
| 8 | 53-65 | "Linked Macs. New in Omni zero point sixteen." | The mole waves; "Linked Macs" title, "Omni 0.16 · hanxiao.io/omni". Final chord. | What it is called and where to get it. |

Rules: a headline holds still while it is read; one thing moves at a time; springs settle before the
line that depends on them is read; app footage is never cropped so that UI text is cut; no person,
no private file in any visible result.

Measured on the chosen score (music/score-2.mp3, librosa): beat 0.418 s, groove from ~10 s, breakdown
36.1-46.2, return 46.3, last hit 63.4 then silence. Narration (chosen takes at 1.1x) totals 44.7 s.
Between "agents" and "end", 53-58: the app montage, no narration.
