# Linked Macs - storyboard

76 seconds, 1920x1080, 60 fps. The launch film's world (cut paper, the mole, Sadaltager at 1.1x,
a Lyria score), real app footage for every claim about the app. Times are approximate until the
narration is placed; each beat says what the viewer learns.

| # | Time | Narration | Picture | The viewer learns |
|---|------|-----------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | 0-8 | "Omni already searches every kind of file on your Mac, by meaning. But what if your files are spread across several Macs?" | Close on the Mac Studio's desk as files of every kind land on it; on "but" the camera pulls back to the second desk, the MacBook Pro, and the two moles in their own burrows. | What Omni already does, and the question: files on several Macs. |
| 2 | 9-17 | "Omni can now link them. Open Link a Mac on both, and type the six digits one shows into the other." | Two paper Macs whose screens are the real Link a Mac sheets: the code on one, ringed as it is spoken, typed into the other, then "Linked". | How to link: one window on each Mac, six digits. |
| 3 | 17-28 | "The code proves the two Macs are talking to each other... on your network, or over your VPN." | Underground: a tunnel dug between the burrows, a seal on it, the cloud sent away. Chips: "TLS 1.3 · keys pinned at pairing", "your network / or your VPN". | It is private and needs no server. |
| 4 | 28-39 | "From then on, one search covers every linked Mac..." | A query travels the tunnel, both moles dig ("own index · own GPU"), photos come back. Push into the real app: MacBook Pro's fjord first, among this Mac's results. | One search, every Mac, each on its own compute. |
| 5 | 39-46 | "Link as many Macs as you like. One search fans out to all of them at once, and every answer comes back to you." | Pull out from this Mac's burrow to a colony of seventeen; a tunnel to each, a mole in each; the query fans out, every burrow digs, photos stream back to the middle. | Federated: any number of Macs. |
| 6 | 46-51 | "If a Mac is asleep, the search simply goes on without it." | The score breaks down. Back to the two burrows: the far mole asleep, the near one keeps working. | An offline Mac never blocks a search. |
| 7 | 51-58 | "Open, preview or drag a result, and the file comes across the link. Its folders are in your sidebar." | The groove returns as the far mole wakes; a photo crosses the tunnel. Real app: the sidebar's Linked Macs, MacBook Pro › Iceland. | Remote files behave like local ones. |
| 8 | 60-63 | "Local agents get the same reach, through the same tools." | A terminal card: real `omni search` output with an "On MacBook Pro:" section; held until read. | Agents and the command line see linked Macs too. |
| 9 | 68-76 | "Omni Linked Macs: multimodal search, now federated." | The mole waves; "Linked Macs", "Federated search across your Macs.", "New in Omni 0.16 · hanxiao.io/omni". Final chord, natural decay. | What it is called and where to get it. |

Rules: a headline holds still while it is read; one thing moves at a time; springs settle before the
line that depends on them is read; app footage is never cropped so that UI text is cut; no person,
no private file in any visible result.

Measured on the chosen score (music/score78-0.mp3, librosa): beat 0.592 s, groove from ~18 s, bass
out at 46.1 (breakdown), back at 57.1, final chord 73.1, decayed to -47 dB by 76. A first edit
looped eight bars of the 62 s score instead; the loop landed in a quieter part of the arrangement
(4 dB down, 10 dB less bass) and sounded like a different piece, so the score was regenerated at
length. Between "agents" and "end", 64-68: the app montage, no narration.
